What we measure, and why it takes two modes

The four numbers PS-036 asks for, who pays for each, how the offline harness and the simulated lab measure them, what each mode cannot tell you, and which claims the results actually support.

This page explains the method; it contains no benchmark results. The measured numbers are in each run's report.html and in the dashboard's Compare tile, labelled measured, estimated or analytical.
Sizes are approximate: one signed A answer ≈ 113 bytes of DNS plus the signature. MTL sizes are the maximum-size formulas of draft-harvey-cfrg-mtl-mode for a 10,005-RRset zone (condensed ≤ 284 B, full signature ≈ 8.7 KB). "About 0.6 s per SLH-DSA signature on one core" is the methodology's stated figure.
Stacks: the offline harness uses the latest profile (FIPS 205 SLH-DSA, liboqs 0.16); the simulated lab uses draft06 (SPHINCS+-SHA2-128s-simple, liboqs 0.14, same sizes). Details: docs/methodology.md and docs/scope-and-limits.md.
